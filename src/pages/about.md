# about

**Elle Gamble** is a Glaceon, music maker, artist, and programmer. She uses it/she pronouns, and is non-binary. She primarily uses VSCodium, and likes to make IDM. She is not robot-adjacent or therian, nor does she want to be dehumanised or treated like an object.

**Elle** is part of the 2311 system, and was wholly diverged from [Sven](https://sven.llia.workers.dev/about/). The source code and license for this site can be found [here](https://tangled.org/llia.cc/elle).

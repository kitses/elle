## [Lucy Bedroque - Unmusique](https://soundcloud.com/lucybedroque/sets/unmusique) *(rage, digicore)*{.small}

**A** &mdash; Being considered Lucy’s "debut album", whatever that may mean, *Unmusique* is one of the few albums I’ve listened to where it was actual love at first sight&mdash;you can just immediately call a gem, even if there are a few drop-off points.

*Unmusique* starts by immediately overstimulating you, then moves on to&mdash;according to [last.fm users](https://www.last.fm/music/Lucy+Bedroque/_/g6+anthem/+shoutbox?sort=popular)&mdash;the "greatest song ever made", "peak", or an "eargasm *\[sic\]*". And they are correct to think that. *Ultraviolet* *(with prettifun)*{.small} is a fucking song where I can’t tell the difference between who’s rapping because they both sound the same, mostly. *2010 Justin Bieber* lives up to its name too well for me to editorialize about, and *Smackdown*, *Fenty Face*, and *Ouija* are all aggressive to a quite oppressive degree&mdash;to the point where if I listen enough, Lucy might crack my fucking skull open.

*Made In Italy*, *Cara Mia*, and *Ignorant* are all disgustingly beautiful songs that you can do a gay little dance in your room to&mdash;*Tout Naturel* just exists, and *I Am Impossible* is the "sybau national anthem", according to one of my associates (ffuckin’ Aspen). I personally don’t like *One Of Us Is Lying*, despite having it loved on last.fm. The title track, *Unmusique*, is one I’m rather unenthusiastic about. *Cat’s Eye* would make me cry if I was employed or 14 years old&mdash;I say this positively. And finally, *Yes, You May* goes in the same category as *2010 Justin Bieber*.

I forgot to write this in pre-publishing, so I'll put it here. jackzebra on *Finish Him* got done so dirty with the mixing. His rapping on it is a bit wack, regardless.

Good album. Go listen to it. Do not trust any of my opinions, *<u>ever</u>*.

## [Oklou - Choke Enough](https://oklou.bandcamp.com/album/choke-enough) *(art pop, ambient)*{.small}

**B+** &mdash; Once again, another debut album from an artist, *Choke Enough* is the type of album where I’d rather just listen to the tracks individually instead of listening to the album on repeat through its entire length. I will now proceed to dog on this album by putting each of its tracks in a list and whining about them all instead of writing paragraphs.

1.  Endless *(co-produced with Danny L. Harle)*{.small}

    Milquetoast, about what you’d expect and want for the first track on an album.

2.  Thank You for Recording

    Ear-grating. Immediately breaks my rule of "the second track *must* be able to get viably released as a single". This is the exact opposite&mdash;a runaway out-take. A.G. Cook was a writer on this, too!

3.  Family and Friends

    Cinematic-esque, I’d feign. Definitely 2 storeys up from the previous track in terms of goodness.

4.  Obvious

    Club music played by the local bard, or a song that’ll make you "feel like a cobra being forced to dance by a snake charmer"&mdash;both unusually accurate and correct descriptions that were put on the [last.fm page](https://www.last.fm/music/Oklou/_/obvious/+shoutbox?sort=popular) for this song. I had 21 scrobbles on this, behind the next’s 20.

5.  Ict

    Another one of those songs you can do a gay little dance to. The title is rumoured to be an acronym for "Ice Cream Truck"&mdash;which I doubt, but would like to be true.

6.  Choke Enough *(co-produced with Lucien Krampf)*{.small}

    The title track. It’s alright, I guess. The second part is pretty catchy. Not much to say.

7.  "(;´༎ຶٹ༎ຶ‘)" *(that’s the actual name of this track)*{.small}

    The only track on this album that’s been solo-produced by Oklou&mdash;a brief intermission. Not much to say, also.

8.  Take Me by the Hand *(with Bladee, co-produced with Danny L. Harle)*{.small}

    Another one of those tracks, Oklou sings some catchy stuff and Bladee sounds like he’s been put through *Ableton Live*’s Warp time-stretching in most parts. Because he likely has been.

9.  Plague Dogs

    Cinematic; I keep having this song stuck in my head, but it’s pretty average.

10. Forces

    Intermission, not worth mentioning. I’m only putting this here to line the track numbers up.

11. Harvest Sky *(with and co-produced by Underscores, co-produced with Nick Léon)*

    I am currently listening to this, and I’m resisting the urge to break out in dance. I do not live alone. Thank you, Underscores and Oklou.

12. Want to Wanna Come Back *(co-produced with Danny L. Harle)*{.small}

    Amen break used. Pretty alright. Not much to say.

13. Blade Bird

    The ending track. Average. I do not care for this.

I think I get what Anthony Fantano means now by him saying that this is "not immersive" (somewhat) and "just wallpaper" after bothering to listen to the album in full. It’s... just okay. I can’t articulate my feelings well for this.

## [Proem - Until Here For Years](https://proem.bandcamp.com/album/until-here-for-years) *(IDM)*{.small}

**A+** &mdash; If you know me or my system personally&mdash;in which case, no, you *don’t*, nobody does&mdash;you may be aware that I am an absolute sucker for IDM (or as I would like to call it currently, "braindance", but the album cover dictates else). This is the culmination of all that. Beautiful organized chaos, incarnate. I do not like wasting my fingers on this genre most-of-the-time as the tracks speak for themselves, so I won’t, but if you’re into this type of stuff, *please* listen. This earns the *e2l2* and *2311* joint seal of approval.
